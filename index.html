    A little
    about me.

    I’m interested in how complex systems behave, and what it takes to make them reliable.

    I study Statistics and Data Science at UBC. What I've come to enjoy most isn't just finding solutions to problems, but understanding why those solutions work, what assumptions they rely on, and where they fall apart.

    That curiosity has taken me beyond the mathematics. I spent a year at Sanofi working on data platforms and cloud infrastructure, building and maintaining the systems that move and process data. It gave me a different perspective on computing, one where reliability, architecture, and the details of implementation matter just as much as the ideas behind them.

    These days, I'm particularly interested in machine learning, distributed systems, and the engineering that makes modern AI possible. As I figure out exactly where I want to go with those interests, I enjoy the process of taking something apart intellectually, understanding it properly, and putting that understanding to use.
